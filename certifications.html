Global Quality Assurance Certification

FM Approvals is the global leader in third-party testing. Their Standard 2510 is the industry benchmark for flood mitigation performance. FloodShield barriers are rigorously tested to resist continuous wave action, extreme hydrostatic pressure, and high-velocity debris impact with near-zero leakage.

  • Hydrostatic Pressure Resistance: Seals hold firm under maximum flood depths without leakage.
  • Dynamic Impact Resilience: Prevents structural breach when hit by high-velocity, floating debris.
  • Near-Zero Leakage Verification: Maintains a highly restrictive seal to keep water ingress below critical thresholds.
